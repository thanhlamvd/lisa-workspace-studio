Privacy Policy — Lisa Workspace Studio

Effective date: November 3, 2026

1. Who this policy covers

Lisa Workspace Studio is a single-creator publishing tool for the TikTok channel @lisa.nguyn50. It is not a public social network, and it does not collect information from TikTok viewers.

2. Information we collect

When the creator connects their TikTok account, we may receive, through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API only:

  • Basic TikTok profile information authorized by the creator, such as display name, username, and avatar.
  • The video file, title, caption, hashtags, and publishing settings the creator provides for a specific post.
  • Basic technical information needed to complete an upload, such as upload status and error messages returned by TikTok.

We do not collect:

  • The creator's TikTok password.
  • Viewers' personal information, followers' lists, or private messages.
  • Payment card information.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to:

  • Authenticate the creator's TikTok account.
  • Upload or publish the creator's approved video, with AI-generated content labelled.
  • Show upload and publishing status to the creator.
  • Diagnose a failed upload.

We do not use creator information for advertising, and we do not sell personal information.

4. Sharing

Information is shared only with TikTok, through its official APIs, and only for the upload or publication the creator authorized. We do not share information with other third parties, except where required by law.

5. Storage and retention

  • Video files and captions are kept only as long as needed to complete the authorized upload or publication, and for a short period afterward to confirm the result.
  • Access tokens are stored securely and are not shared in chat, screenshots, or public files.
  • When the creator revokes TikTok authorization, or asks for deletion, stored tokens and unpublished drafts held by the tool are deleted, except for records TikTok itself retains under its own policies.

6. Security

Access is limited to the creator's own account authorization. No password is stored. If a credential is ever exposed, it must be revoked and replaced through TikTok's official settings, not shared in chat.

7. Children's privacy

Lisa Workspace Studio is intended for the adult creator who owns the connected TikTok account. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same Privacy Policy address, with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the TikTok channel @lisa.nguyn50.
